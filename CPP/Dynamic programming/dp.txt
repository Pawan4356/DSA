Memoization - Top down

Steps:
1) Declare an array; (Not required everytime, sometimes variables are enough!!)
2) Store the answer of every sub-problem;
3) Checking if the sub-problem was previously solved;

Tabulation - Bottom up

Identification:
- Number of ways
- All possible ways
- Best way
- Min-Max

Approach:
- Try to represent the problem in terms of index
- Do all possible stuff on that index according to the problem statement
- Count all ways - sum of all stuffs
  Minimum - minimum of all stuffs
  Maximum - maximum of all stuffs
